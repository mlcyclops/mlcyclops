## <img src="assets/icons/mission.svg" width="28" align="top" alt=""> Mission Brief

> **Proof is the system that makes advanced technology dependable.**

I've spent 26+ years taking technology from *demo* to *fielded* - starting as an enlisted network
operator and ending up the senior Chief Warrant Officer of an Army AI Detachment. Along the way:

- <img src="assets/icons/satellite.svg" width="18" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Fielded the Army's first AI-powered language translation capability** in wartime Iraq (2006) - Arabic broadcast TV to English in under a second, networked across a 350+ soldier task force.
- <img src="assets/icons/cloudlock.svg" width="18" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Accredited a first-of-its-kind Google Cloud enclave at Impact Level 5** for a U.S. Air Force customer - GKE, identity, and collaboration services where others said it couldn't be done.
- <img src="assets/icons/agent.svg" width="18" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Principal Investigator** for an Army advanced R&D program building **AI agents for predictive logistics** on Gemini/Gemma, with knowledge-graph platforms running on edge hardware.
- <img src="assets/icons/vision.svg" width="18" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Deployed containerized computer vision to the tactical edge** with Google while architecting cloud-to-edge for U.S. Special Operations Command - plus Zero Trust, data-science platforms, and IaC in denied/degraded/disconnected environments.
- <img src="assets/icons/rocket.svg" width="18" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Founded [TECHLEAD 187 LLC](https://lucid-agent.web.app)** (2026) - a Tampa engineering practice for security-first agentic AI, from national security to commercial verticals.
